Solar explorer planet surfaces and landing-site data (pt1837u, Nov 24 2026).
All imagery is U.S. government public domain (NASA, JPL, USGS, Goddard, ASU,
JHUAPL) except where a generated stand-in is noted. Each file was resized and
recompressed; metadata was stripped. No non-commercial sources.

saturn.jpg
  No official public-domain 2k cylindrical color map of Saturn's atmosphere
  was available to this build. This is a generated latitude-band texture
  painted in natural-color tones sampled from Cassini portraits
  (NASA / JPL / SSI). It is a stand-in, not a projected Cassini mosaic.
  The north-pole hexagon is a separate overlay (see saturn_hex.webp).

saturn_hex.webp
  Source: "In Full View: Saturn Streaming Hexagon", PIA17652, Cassini.
  A false-color polar view of the north-pole hexagon, cropped to a circular
  overlay with a transparent edge.
  https://images-assets.nasa.gov/image/PIA17652/PIA17652~orig.jpg
  Author: NASA / JPL / SSI
  License: Public domain (U.S. government work)

uranus.jpg
  A generated smooth texture in Voyager 2 true-color tones sampled from the
  left (true-color) half of PIA00032, "Uranus in True and False Color."
  NASA / JPL. Public domain. Not a cylindrical projection of the Voyager
  frames; Uranus showed very few visible features at Voyager's resolution.

neptune.jpg
  A generated smooth texture in Voyager 2 true-color tones. NASA / JPL.
  Public domain. The Great Dark Spot was a transient storm in 1989 and is
  not drawn here as if it were still present.

sites.json
  Landing-site coordinates and mission facts from NASA NSSDC spacecraft
  records (public U.S. government information). Links are in each record.
  Great Red Spot position is measured on PIA07782 (Cassini, Dec 2000).
