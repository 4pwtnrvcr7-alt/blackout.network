Live Broadcasts — wire, local TV and live radio transcripts

Audio broadcasts play right here: press ▶ Play on a card (only one plays at a time; starting another stops the first). They play the station's live stream now; the transcribed 60-second clip itself isn't archived. These are live Icecast MP3 streams with no server rewind window, so the scrubber rewinds and fast-forwards within what your browser has buffered since you pressed Play, and ⏭ Jump to live returns to the live edge. Text items are news articles and open their source in a new tab. Informational only: never changes threat scores.
